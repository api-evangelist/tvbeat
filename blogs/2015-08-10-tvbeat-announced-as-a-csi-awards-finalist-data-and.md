We are extremely excited to be shortlisted in the Best Data and Analytics Innovation category. The CSI Awards, which are celebrating their 13th year, have experienced the highest number of entries on record, making it an even greater achievement to be shortlisted this year. The winners will be determined by an independent judging panel and will be announced on Friday 11 September 2015 at IBC.
