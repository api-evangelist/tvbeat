London, United Kingdom & Odessa, Ukraine – 16 th April 2015 TVbeat, a TV analytics company delivering real-time TV audience measurement solutions today announced a new partnership with Infomir, a company specializing in manufacturing devices for interactive television service providers. Customers of both companies benefit from this partnership, as they can easily deliver more targeted content covering any business model, anywhere and on any device. Infomir offers a comprehensive range of products and services that are developed according to their customer’s needs.
