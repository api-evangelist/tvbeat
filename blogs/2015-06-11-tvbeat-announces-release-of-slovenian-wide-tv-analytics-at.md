Local and International broadcasters can now access census based analytics to measure viewing, optimize scheduling and avoid zero-ratings in Slovenia. TVbeat, the next generation TV analytics company delivering real-time TV audience measurement solutions today announced the launch of their Slovenian dataset at New European Market (NEM). This announcement comes shortly after the launch of their Croatian dataset to broadcasters and agencies.
