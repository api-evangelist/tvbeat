LONDON – 22nd January 2015 - TVbeat has appointed Ana Jelavic as Client Services Manager in the Adriatic Region. Ana will work closely with the sales team to ensure that all client’s needs are being met and understood. Additionally, Ana will use her background in research to survey and analyze the Adriatic markets.
